# Kairos

*Greek: "the right moment."*

Kairos is a self-hosted, AI-assisted research tool for US stocks and ETFs. It reads recent news, SEC filings and market data for a ticker and gives you a short, **sourced reading**: a lean (bullish / neutral / bearish) with a confidence level, what's driving it, the bull and bear case, and price context computed from real data.

> **Kairos is a research tool, not financial advice.** Its readings are one input to your own research. Every claim links to its source so you can check it.
