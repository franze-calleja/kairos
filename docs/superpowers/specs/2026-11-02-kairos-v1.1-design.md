# Kairos v1.1 — Design: exposure, reasons, evening refresh, compare

*Date: 2026-11-02 · Status: draft for review · Builds on `2026-11-02-kairos-design.md` (v1)*

Items marked **(owner)** were decided by the owner; **(designer's call)** were chosen by the designer and can be revisited.

## 1. Intent

v1 answers "what's going on with this ticker?". v1.1 answers four follow-up questions a long-term holder of overlapping ETFs and stocks keeps asking:

1. **What do I actually own?** VOO, QQQ and individual mega-caps overlap heavily; the owner can't see their real exposure anywhere.
2. **Is my reason for holding still true?** Long-term investing is about checking the thesis, not the daily price.
3. **What changed before the US opens?** The 07:00 refresh is ~14 hours old by the Manila evening, when buy decisions happen.
4. **Which of these two is better for me?** E.g. QQQ vs SMH, VOO vs SCHD.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals:** the four features below, consistent with redesign A, tested like v1.

**Non-goals:** broker sync or importing positions; cost basis, P&L or tax; full ETF holdings beyond what Yahoo provides (top 10); Discord/VPS (postponed by owner); rebalancing advice.

**Anti-goals (carried from v1, plus):**
- Share counts and portfolio values **never** leave the machine and never appear in AI prompts.
- No buy/sell instructions; exposure is shown, never "you should trim X".
- Exposure must be honest about what it can't see: the part of each ETF beyond its top 10 holdings is shown as "rest of fund", never silently dropped or redistributed.

## 3. Feature A — True exposure

### What the owner sees
A new **Exposure** view (top-bar button, next to Track record):
- **Positions editor:** each holding with a share-count field (fractional allowed) **(owner: share counts)**, latest price and current value, plus total portfolio value.
- **What you really own:** a table of underlying companies, combining direct shares with look-through via each ETF's top holdings. Each row shows total weight in the portfolio, split into "direct" and "via ETFs" (e.g. NVDA 14.2% = 8.0% direct + 3.9% via QQQ + 2.3% via VOO). Top 15 rows, plus a **"Rest of funds (beyond top 10 holdings)"** row with its combined weight.
- **By sector:** horizontal bars of portfolio weight per sector (ETFs via Yahoo sector weightings, stocks via their own sector).
- Empty state when no share counts are entered: explains what to fill in.

### Rules (designer's call)
- **Value** = shares × latest price. Price from the ticker's latest stored reading when it's younger than 24h, otherwise from Yahoo `info` (`regularMarketPrice` or `previousClose`). A holding with no price shows "price unavailable" and is excluded from totals, with a note.
- **ETF or stock:** from the latest reading's `kind`, otherwise Yahoo `info.quoteType`.
- **Look-through:** for each ETF holding, each top holding gets `etf_value × holding_weight`; the ETF's remaining `1 − Σ(top weights)` goes to "rest of funds". Stocks count 100% direct. Underlying symbols are normalized (e.g. GOOG and GOOGL stay separate rows, since they're separate securities; the table shows the company name).
- **Sectors:** ETF sector weightings come from `funds_data.sector_weightings` (snake_case keys mapped to Yahoo's display names, e.g. `consumer_cyclical` → "Consumer Cyclical"); a stock's whole value goes to its `info.sector`. Missing sector data goes to "Unknown".
- ETF holdings and sector data are cached in memory for 6 hours (they change quarterly).
- Shares are stored in SQLite (`lists.shares`, nullable, only meaningful for holdings), validated ≥ 0; 0 or empty clears it.

### API (designer's call)
- `PUT /api/lists/holdings/{ticker}/shares` body `{"shares": number | null}` → updated list entry.
- `GET /api/exposure` → `{total_value, positions: [{ticker, shares, price, value, weight}], underlying: [{symbol, name, weight, direct, via: {ETF: weight}}], rest_of_funds: weight, sectors: [{sector, weight}], missing_prices: [ticker]}`.

## 4. Feature B — Your reason for holding (thesis check)

### What the owner sees
- On each reading, a **"Your reason"** card at the top of the right column: an editable one-line reason (max 200 characters) and, once checked, a verdict badge (**Supports ✓**, **Challenges ⚠**, **Mixed**, **No clear signal**) with a one-to-two sentence explanation that cites sources like every other claim.
- Works for holdings *and* watched tickers (a reason for watching is just as useful) **(designer's call)**.
- The overview table gets a small **⚠** marker on rows whose latest check says *Challenges*.
- After editing the reason, the card says "Refresh to check your updated reason" until the next reading is built.

### Rules
- The reason text is sent with that ticker's Gemini request **(owner: yes)**: only the sentence, phrased as "a thesis to evaluate". No share counts, values or other tickers. Same request, no extra AI cost.
- `AISummary` gains an optional `thesis_check: {verdict, text, source_ids}`. Validation as for other claims: if `source_ids` aren't all supplied ids, the explanation is dropped and the verdict becomes `no_signal`. `no_signal` needs no citations.
- When no reason is set, the prompt has no thesis section and `thesis_check` is absent.
- The reading stores the reason text it checked (`thesis_checked`), so the UI can tell whether the check is current.
- Stored in `lists.thesis` (nullable). API: `PUT /api/lists/{list}/{ticker}/thesis` body `{"thesis": string | null}`; list entries gain `thesis` and `thesis_verdict`.

## 5. Feature C — Evening refresh before the US open

- A second daily run at **21:00 Manila by default** (`EVENING_REFRESH_TIME`, empty disables) **(designer's call)**. In Manila that's after pre-market earnings and right around the US open (21:30/22:30 depending on US daylight saving).
- **Readings only** for holdings + watching (no Discover scan, no track-record outcomes). The 8–10 minute S&P 500 scan stays a morning job. About 9 extra AI requests per day.
- **Make-up behaviour per slot:** a slot is due when its time has passed, no run was attempted since, and the next slot hasn't started. Morning is due from 07:00 until 21:00; evening from 21:00 until midnight. Opening the laptop at 23:00 runs the evening refresh; opening it at 08:00 the next day runs only the morning one.
- The progress indicator and the Settings page name which refresh is running ("Evening refresh 3 of 9: AAPL…"). The manual button keeps running the full morning job.
- New meta key `last_evening_attempt`; config validated like `MORNING_REFRESH_TIME`.

## 6. Feature D — Side-by-side compare

### What the owner sees
- A **Compare** view (top-bar button, and a "compare with…" link on each reading that pre-fills ticker A).
- Two ticker inputs; then two columns from the two readings (cached readings are reused; an uncached ticker costs one reading, same as opening it).
- **Rows:** price and day change; lean, confidence and takeaway; add-more label, vs 200-day, from 52-week high; 30-day volatility; **1-year return** (from price history); stocks: market cap, P/E vs sector, revenue growth; ETFs: expense ratio and fund size; next earnings; hype status; your reason's verdict if set.
- **Relative performance chart:** both tickers rebased to 100 at the start of the shared period, on one chart, using the 1M/6M/1Y range buttons.
- **For two ETFs:** top-holdings overlap, `Σ min(weight_A, weight_B)` over shared top holdings, with the shared names listed. Labelled "overlap among top 10 holdings", since that's all Yahoo provides.

### Rules (designer's call)
- Frontend-only: no new endpoint, computed from the two `Reading` payloads.
- The "better" column isn't declared; each row highlights the more favourable value only where "favourable" is unambiguous (lower expense ratio, lower volatility). Others are just shown side by side.

## 7. Data & migrations
- `lists` gains nullable `shares REAL` and `thesis TEXT`, added at startup if missing (`ALTER TABLE ... ADD COLUMN`); existing databases upgrade in place.
- Reading payload gains optional `thesis_checked`; `AISummary` gains optional `thesis_check`. Older payloads still load (fields default to None).

## 8. Privacy & security
- Share counts, values and the exposure view are local-only; a test asserts they never appear in a prompt.
- The thesis sentence is the only new data sent to Gemini, and only for its own ticker. The README privacy section is updated to say so.
- New write endpoints validate input (ticker normalization, shares ≥ 0 and finite, thesis ≤ 200 characters, trimmed).

## 9. Left to the builder
- Component structure, styling within redesign A, chart implementation details for the rebased comparison.
- Exact prompt wording for the thesis check (must keep: evaluate only against supplied articles, cite ids, allow no_signal).
- Cache implementation for ETF holdings and sector data.

## 10. Known limits
- Look-through sees only each ETF's top 10 holdings (roughly 35–60% of VOO/QQQ); the rest is shown as one bucket.
- Sector weightings and top holdings come from Yahoo and lag the funds' real composition by up to a quarter.
- The thesis check is only as good as the week's news; "No clear signal" will be common, and that is the honest answer.
